What happens after you sign in, in the order the app does it (design §5.14 F-1 → F-7, F-18). Each step carries its ids into the next. Every call goes to the real API (the banner names it) and everything is saved: a workspace you create shows up in the picker, and an invite sends a real email — read it in the dev inbox.
