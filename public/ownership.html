Handing a workspace over is an offer: its OWNER confirms their password, names a member and types the workspace's name; the member is mailed /ownership#<token> and owns it once they accept. One offer is open at a time, and offers are limited to 5 per 15 minutes per person, refusals included. The rescue at the bottom is separate: the organization's OWNER gives a workspace with no owner at all its owner.

Accept an offer — POST /ownership-transfers/accept

The emailed link is /ownership#<token>; the token is read from after the # and posted in the body. You must be signed in as the person it was offered to: signed out is refused 401, anyone else gets NOT_FOUND. Every refusal leaves the offer open.

Open offer — GET /workspaces/:id/ownership-transfer

Only the workspace's OWNER may read, make or withdraw its offer (capability workspace.transfer); anyone else gets 403. An offer past its 24 hours still shows PENDING until the expiry job closes it. Withdrawing is safe to repeat and needs no identity confirmation.

Confirm it is you — POST /auth/identity/confirm

An offer and a rescue each spend one WORKSPACE_OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER grant: tied to this session, good for 5 minutes. A wrong name, a missing seat or an owned workspace is refused before the grant is spent; an offer refused for one already open spends it.

Offer it — POST /workspaces/:id/ownership-transfer

Nothing changes hands yet. The member must have an ACTIVE membership here and a seat in the organization. To offer it to someone else, withdraw the open offer first.

Rescue — POST /workspaces/:id/ownership/assign

For a workspace with no owner at all, e.g. after its last owner left. Only the organization's OWNER may; refused WORKSPACE_HAS_OWNER while it has one. Needs the identity confirmation above. The person must hold a seat in the organization; they become OWNER here in one step.
