Removing someone — OWNER or ADMIN

Press impact on a row first: it says what the removal would do. Removal signs them out of every session they have. The key is renewed after each removal, since each row is a new request.

Confirm your password — POST /auth/identity/confirm

Grants one handover (ORG_OWNERSHIP_TRANSFER) for 5 minutes, on this session only. The handover spends it even if it is then refused.

Leave this organization — POST /organizations/:orgId/members/me/leave

You stay signed in. The owner cannot leave, nor can anyone who still owns a workspace here (LAST_OWNER).

Workspaces — GET /organizations/:orgId/workspaces

Every workspace in the organization, newest first — including ones you are not in. use makes a row the current workspace for the other pages, when your switcher has it.
