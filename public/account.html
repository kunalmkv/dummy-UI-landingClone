Mail is never really sent locally — it waits in the outbox. Read codes and links from the dev inbox. Use @example.com addresses.

Register

POST /auth/account/register — creates the account (PENDING_VERIFICATION) and its organization, and mails a 6-digit code plus a link. It cannot sign in until verified.

Sign in

POST /auth/session/login — an unverified account is refused with 403. A wrong password answers 401 with attemptsRemaining; five in 15 minutes locks the address.

Sign in without a password

Both answer identically whether or not the address has an account — try a made-up one. The emailed links open /reset-password-tester and /magic-link-tester.

Profile

GET /auth/profile/get — the canonical user object. The cookie is HttpOnly, so only the server can say who you are.

Change password

POST /auth/password/change — signs out every other device; this one stays signed in.

Sign out

POST /auth/session/logout ends this device's session and works even when already signed out. POST /auth/session/logout-all ends every session, this one included.
