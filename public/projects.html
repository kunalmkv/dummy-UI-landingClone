People — GET /workspaces/:id/projects/:projectId/members

Who reaches the selected project: the admins (via ADMIN) and the people placed on it (via PROJECT_ROLE). Anyone on the project may read it. Placing, changing and removing need an admin; an admin's own entry cannot be changed here.

A member of the workspace, at EDITOR or READER. A first EDITOR role takes a paid seat. Not in the workspace yet? Invite them below.
