Everything here acts on the workspace the picker holds. The workspace loads on its own when the picker changes; every other section loads when you ask.

The workspace

GET /workspaces/:workspaceId — what it is, and what you may do in it. Its version is what the edit form sends back as If-Match.

Edit settings

PATCH /workspaces/:workspaceId. Only the fields you changed are sent. If-Match is the version you last read — if anyone changed the workspace since (a branding save counts too), the server answers VERSION_CONFLICT. Edit If-Match by hand to force one.

Is a slug free?

GET /workspaces/slug-available?slug= — a taken, reserved or malformed slug comes back with a free one to offer. A workspace's own slug reads as taken.

Roles

GET /workspaces/:workspaceId/roles — the matrix the server enforces. partial means only on what that role reaches or owns.

My capabilities

GET /workspaces/:workspaceId/me/capabilities — what you may do here right now, and which projects you reach.

Visit

POST /workspaces/:workspaceId/visit — a page calls this when the workspace opens. The workspace's lastVisit then names you; your last-seen time moves at most once a minute.

Apps

GET /workspaces/:workspaceId/apps — each app's state for you. Joining a waitlist asks again safely; load again to see onWaitlist.

Branding

GET and PUT /workspaces/:workspaceId/branding. The colour must be #rrggbb. Turning the badge off needs a plan that allows it. The logo is refused until uploads exist. Saving moves the workspace's version.

Command palette

GET /workspaces/:workspaceId/search?q= — projects, members and pages whose name starts with what you type. At least 2 characters; the page lets you send fewer so the refusal can be seen.

Resume and health

GET /workspaces/:workspaceId/resume — where you left off (empty until a module adds items). GET /workspaces/:workspaceId/health — how the connections are doing.

Danger zone — archive and restore

Archiving makes the workspace read-only and frees its seats. It needs a fresh password check first (POST /auth/identity/confirm with action WORKSPACE_ARCHIVE), good for one archive within 5 minutes. Restore needs no identity confirmation. Afterwards, load the workspace — and reload the picker's list — to see the new state.
